# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary users are technical recruiters, engineering hiring managers, and team leads on international or remote engineering teams looking to hire for Junior to Mid-level Backend or Full-Stack developer roles (Node.js, NestJS, C#, TypeScript, SQL). Secondary users include local or regional engineering teams in Latin America and prospective freelance clients seeking reliable web and API solutions.

## Product Purpose

The portfolio exists to provide clear, indisputable proof of Rosendo Garcia's software engineering capabilities, work ethic, and communication skills. Success means converting visiting recruiters and hiring managers into interview invitations and direct contact.

## Positioning

A backend-focused software developer who pairs technical discipline (RESTful API architecture, NestJS/Node.js, C#, relational databases, and real-world system maintenance) with proven bilingual English fluency and customer-facing maturity developed through live support operations for U.S. platforms.

## Operating Context

- Evaluated primarily by busy reviewers spending 30 to 90 seconds scanning candidate qualifications.
- Accessed across desktop and mobile devices via links from LinkedIn, resumes, job applications, and GitHub profiles.
- Operates in international recruiting pipelines where English fluency, timezone flexibility, and dependable async communication are primary hiring criteria.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Static Generation & Hosting:** Built with Astro 5 as a high-performance static site deployed to Vercel.
- **Bilingual Localization (i18n):** Full support for English (`/`) and Spanish (`/es/`) with static JSON dictionaries (`src/components/i18n/{en,es}.json`).
- **Theme Support:** Clean light and dark modes with persistent state toggle.
- **Direct Contact & Verification:** Working contact form, one-click email copy helper with toast alerts, and direct CV resume downloads.
- **Zero Fabrication:** Only verifiable projects, real technical skills, and genuine career history are featured.

## Product Principles

1. **Proof Over Assertions:** Every skill claim links directly to runnable code, public GitHub repositories, verified credentials, or real-world production track records.
2. **Dual-Competency Differentiation:** Make the intersection of backend engineering depth and bilingual customer-facing communication immediately apparent.
3. **Low-Friction Evaluation:** Enable reviewers to inspect core competencies, live demonstrations, resumes, and contact methods within seconds without navigation friction.
4. **Authentic Narrative:** Present honest career progression and continuous learning with transparency, dignity, and technical rigor.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Target WCAG 2.2 AA conformance across all viewports.
- Clear semantic landmarks, skip-to-main-content navigation link, and keyboard accessibility for all interactive triggers.
- Accessible contrast ratios maintained across both light and dark modes.
- Respect user motion preferences (`prefers-reduced-motion`).
